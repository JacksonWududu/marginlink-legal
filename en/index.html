Privacy Policy

MarginLink is provided by individual developer yanhao wu. We aim to process reading materials on your Mac. Podcast searches, programme resources and audio playback connect to the relevant third parties. This policy explains data handling in the app and on the legal documents website separately.

Privacy contact: BloatMonster@163.com.

1. Local reading materials

When you import an EPUB or PDF, the app copies it into its own local library without modifying the source file you selected. It stores the title, author, format, original filename and information needed for reading, and identifies documents using SHA-256. SHA-256 identifies content; it does not encrypt the file.

Highlights, quotations, notes, topics, colours, source locations, creation and modification times, reading positions, progress and interface preferences are stored locally. The current app has no feature that uploads your library, documents or notes to a developer server, and provides neither app accounts nor app-managed cloud sync.

Text recognition for scanned PDFs uses Apple Vision on the device. Pages are processed locally; quotations and location information that you save can become part of your local notes.

2. Images and the collection wall

Images you choose for the collection wall are stored locally as originals, thumbnails and related files. Originals may retain EXIF and other metadata, including any existing location information. The app does not guarantee that it removes this metadata. Replacing or restoring a framed image may retain the preceding image for restoration.

The current app does not upload these images to the developer. If you share, export or back up materials yourself, the handling of those copies depends on the tools and services you choose.

3. Podcast searches and resource requests

Podcast features make the following network requests:

  • Searches send the search text and selected directory region to Apple's iTunes directory. Looking up an Apple Podcasts link sends the programme ID and region.
  • Previewing or opening a programme requests its RSS feed, artwork and related resources from the publisher or its providers. A preview may also save programme and episode records locally.
  • Reading programme text requests publicly available transcripts supplied by the publisher. Playback requests audio from its media host. Requests may pass through third-party redirects or CDNs.

The directory region selects a search catalogue. Its default may be derived from the interface language or system region; this feature does not use GPS location. The third-party providers receiving requests can ordinarily receive the connecting IP address, request time and resource address, together with search text, region or programme ID included in a request. The app's podcast client also sends an app identifier and audio range requests needed for playback. Its current implementation does not attach your library, notes or images, or attach login cookies or Authorization request headers. A resource URL supplied by a publisher may itself contain an access signature or token.

Third parties may therefore learn which programmes or episodes you request. Their independent handling and retention practices are governed by their own policies and cannot be controlled or guaranteed by this app. See the Apple Privacy Policy. Other programme and media providers publish their own policies.

You can choose not to use podcast features. Features that need network access cannot operate fully while offline.

4. Local podcast records and temporary audio

Programme and episode information, resource URLs, full public transcripts, podcast notes, playback positions, volume and some selection states are stored locally. Artwork is also cached locally. Removing a podcast item from the collection wall does not delete its listening records and notes.

On supported macOS versions, transcript timing alignment uses local audio and already installed dictation language resources through the system SpeechAnalyzer. The current implementation does not request cloud recognition or download speech models for this purpose. Alignment may download an episode and create temporary conversion files. The relevant temporary files are cleaned up when tasks finish normally or the playback loader closes; an abnormal exit may leave residual files.

5. Analytics, diagnostics and security

The current app contains no advertising or analytics SDK and has no implementation that automatically sends usage statistics or crash reports to the developer. The app and operating system may record local diagnostics such as runtime errors. Diagnostics you allow Apple to collect through system settings, store records and materials you send voluntarily are subject to the relevant settings and policies.

The app restricts scripts and remote resources within books and access to arbitrary file paths. Core reading libraries are bundled locally. Podcast requests access external services separately. These measures do not guarantee absolute security. Local data has no app-level encryption; device permissions, disk encryption and backups are managed by you and the operating system.

6. Retention, deletion and data loss

Reading and podcast materials are primarily kept in app-managed local storage. You can delete individual book notes and podcast notes in the interface. The current version has no single control that clears all books and podcast records. Uninstalling the app should not be treated as proof that all support files, temporary files or system backups have been removed.

The current implementation clears and rebuilds the library if its local index cannot be decoded or fails content validation. This may delete book, podcast and collection-wall materials in the same storage directory. The app therefore cannot guarantee that materials remain until you choose to delete them. Keep separate copies of important documents and notes.

For a complete local cleanup, first quit the app and manage its support and temporary files according to your actual installation environment. Contact us if you need help. The developer cannot remotely access or delete materials held only on your device or in your personal backups. Withdrawing access to a selected file does not automatically delete an imported copy. To stop podcast network activity, quit the app.

7. Contacting the developer

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer, investigate issues or handle requests. Please avoid sending unrelated full books, sensitive notes or personal photographs; synthetic examples are preferable where practical. Messages also pass through your email provider and the developer's email provider.

Contact materials are retained for as long as needed to handle the request or as required by applicable law. You can use the email address in this policy to ask about, correct or request deletion of contact materials held by the developer. If retention is legally required, we will explain the relevant circumstances.

8. Legal documents website

This website itself adds no analytics scripts, advertising, login system, tracking cookies or external fonts. The website is intended for GitHub Pages hosting. When you visit that hosted version, GitHub records and stores visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement. This is separate from the app's handling of your local library.

9. Updates and contact

We will update this page when features or data handling change. Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and English versions are intended to have equivalent content. Please contact us if you find an inconsistency. Send privacy questions or requests to BloatMonster@163.com, addressed to yanhao wu.
